Change Your Mood with Scent

Scent is one of the fastest ways to change how you feel because your nose is directly connected to the limbic system, the part of your brain that handles memory and emotions. Unlike sight or sound, which have to be processed by other parts of the brain first, a smell can trigger an immediate shift in your nervous system—lowering stress or sparking a sudden burst of energy. In a world that is often loud and overwhelming, choosing a specific scent allows you to "reset" your mental environment. Whether you need to wash away a long day or find the focus to start a new project, the right fragrance acts like an anchor for your mood.
