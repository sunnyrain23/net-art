Aura Scents

Deep in the woods of Southern France, there lives an old man in a cottage where thousands of people make the journey to witness the magic of making scents. His potions are infamous for changing the way people think and feel. You approach the cottage towered by the trees and a waterfall and open the door to a cozy workshop. Sitting in a desk, he looks up from his book and asks you what type of perfume you wish him to make for you.
