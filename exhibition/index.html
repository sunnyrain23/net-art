Embark on a New World

Curated by Ridhima Biswas

The theme of this collection explores diverse worlds through the power of hypertext narratives, focusing on locations such as Japan, Montreal, the Woods, and Persepolis. This design approach belongs to a broader movement that blends real-world experiences with vibrant storytelling and digital art. Created in the 21st century—as tools for elaborate web design became more accessible—these works utilize beauty and aesthetics to transport the viewer to another place. Each narrative employs stunning graphics and interactive elements to tell stories about specific components of a landscape. Defined by a sense of slowness and ambient audio, these collections eschew minimal game design in favor of dynamic elements that breathe life into simple stories.
